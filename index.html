Comparing Interpolation Methods for Reconstructing Intraday MNQ Futures Prices

Lameck Nyakweba · Mathematics 5670-08, Fall 2026 · Austin Peay State University

Prices are kept every h minutes and the prices in between are hidden. Each method rebuilds them from the kept prices only, and the estimates are compared with the true prices. All the Python code runs inside your browser.
